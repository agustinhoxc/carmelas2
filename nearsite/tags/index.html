Tags describe what a business actually offers: battery storage, evening hours, accessible design. They are written on each business page and collected here, so you can search across categories.

    15 tags across 10 businesses.

    Free estimates 5

    Businesses that will scope your job and quote it without charging for the visit. Some cap this at a service radius or a project type, which is noted on each page.

    Evening hours 4

    Businesses open past 5pm on at least one weekday, for people who cannot take time off during the day.

    Financing available 4

    Businesses that offer payment plans or work with a lending partner so a project does not have to be paid upfront.

    Permit handling 4

    Businesses that file building, electrical or utility paperwork on your behalf instead of leaving it with you. Useful when a project needs city inspection or utility approval before it can be energised or occupied.

    Battery storage 3

    Businesses that size, install or service home and commercial batteries alongside a solar array, including backup wiring for the circuits you want to keep running during an outage.

    Rooftop solar 3

    Photovoltaic arrays mounted on an existing roof rather than on the ground. Roof age, pitch and material all affect what can be installed, so these businesses typically inspect before quoting.

    Spanish-speaking staff 3

    Businesses with staff who handle appointments, quotes and paperwork in Spanish, not only a translated website.

    Google Business Profile 2

    Agencies that set up, verify and maintain the profile that shows your business in Google Maps and local search results.

    Monthly bookkeeping 2

    Ongoing categorisation and reconciliation on a monthly cycle, usually with a set of reports at month end, as opposed to a once-a-year clean-up.

    Older homes 2

    Contractors experienced with pre-war construction: knob-and-tube wiring, plaster walls, settled framing and historic design review.

    Small business taxes 2

    Firms that prepare and file returns for sole proprietors, partnerships, LLCs and S corporations, and that can advise on entity choice and quarterly estimates.

    Website build 2

    Agencies that build or rebuild a website as a fixed-scope project rather than only running campaigns on top of an existing one.

    Accessible design 1

    Work planned around mobility needs: curbless showers, grab bar blocking, door widths and step-free entries.

    Payroll 1

    Running pay cycles, withholding and filings for businesses with employees or contractors.

    Same-day appointments 1

    Practices and businesses that hold slots for urgent cases and can usually see you the day you call.
