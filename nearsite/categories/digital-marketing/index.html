What to ask before you hire

  • Ask who owns the ad accounts, domain and analytics property when the contract ends.
  • Separate the media budget from the agency fee before comparing proposals.
  • For local search, ask what they will change in the first month and how it will be measured.

Questions about digital marketing

What is local SEO?

Work aimed at showing up when people nearby search for what you sell, mostly through your Google Business Profile, your website's location pages and consistent business information across directories.

Is there a minimum contract?

It varies. Each agency states its minimum engagement and notice period on its own page.
