What to ask before you hire

  • Call to confirm your specific plan is accepted, not just the insurer's name.
  • Ask what a first visit includes and whether X-rays are billed separately.
  • If you need an urgent appointment, check the practice's same-day policy rather than assuming.

Questions about dental care

How often should I have a check-up?

Every six months is the common recommendation, adjusted by your dentist based on your history. Practices here list what a routine visit covers.

Can I be seen the same day for pain?

Some practices hold slots for urgent cases. Where that applies, it is noted on the practice's page.
