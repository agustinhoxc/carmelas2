What to ask before you hire

  • Ask for the allowance amounts in the estimate, tile and fixtures included, so two bids can be compared line by line.
  • Confirm who lives with the mess: working hours, dust control and whether the kitchen stays usable.
  • Older houses often need electrical or plumbing brought up to code as part of the permit.

Questions about home renovation

How far ahead should I book a remodeler?

Kitchen and bathroom projects are commonly booked one to three months out. Each contractor lists its current lead time on its own page.

Do I need permits for a bathroom remodel?

Usually yes once plumbing or electrical is moved. Contractors here note whether permit handling is included in their price.
