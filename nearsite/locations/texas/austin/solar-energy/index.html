Questions about solar energy

How long does a residential solar installation take?

The install itself is usually one to three days. The longer part is permitting and utility approval, which the companies here describe on their own pages because it varies by city and utility.

Do I need a battery?

Only if you want backup power during outages or your utility pays little for exported energy. Several companies on this page quote panels and batteries separately so you can compare both.
