About

Front Range Solar Works services photovoltaic systems in Denver and the surrounding Front Range, including systems whose original installer is no longer trading. Roughly half of the work is inspection and repair rather than new installation.

Hail assessment is a regular part of the job here. The company documents module damage in the format insurers ask for, and can carry out the replacement once a claim is settled. New installations are taken on for homes where the roof and electrical service are already in good condition.

Services

  • System inspection and diagnostics

    Production review, string testing and thermal inspection to find the cause of underperformance.

  • Hail and storm damage assessment

    Documented module and racking assessment prepared for an insurance claim, with photographs and serial records.

  • Repair and module replacement

    Replacement of failed panels, optimisers and inverters, including on arrays installed by other companies.

  • Battery retrofit

    Adding storage to an existing array, including compatibility checks on the current inverter.

Questions answered by Front Range Solar Works

My installer went out of business. Can you still help?

Yes. Orphaned systems are a large part of the service work. Manufacturer warranties on the equipment often still apply even when the installer is gone.

How is hail damage assessed?

By module-level inspection with photographs and serial numbers, plus a production comparison before and after the storm, in the format insurers usually request.
