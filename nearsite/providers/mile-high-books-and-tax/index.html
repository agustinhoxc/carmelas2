About

Mile High Books & Tax prepares individual and business returns for people in Denver, with a focus on self-employed clients whose income comes from several places at once: contract work, rentals, a side business.

Appointments run into the evening during filing season, and consultations are available in Spanish. The practice also takes on amended returns and responses to notices when a return prepared elsewhere needs correcting.

Services

  • Individual tax returns

    Federal and Colorado returns, including self-employment income, rental property and multi-state situations.

  • Business returns

    Partnership, LLC and S corporation filings, with quarterly estimates set for the year ahead.

  • Amended returns and notices

    Correcting prior filings and preparing written responses to tax authority notices.

  • Bookkeeping

    Quarterly or monthly bookkeeping for clients who want the return prepared from clean records.

How they work
