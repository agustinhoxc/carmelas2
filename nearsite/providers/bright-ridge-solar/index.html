About

Bright Ridge Solar designs and installs rooftop photovoltaic systems for single-family homes in Austin and the northern suburbs. Every project starts with a roof inspection and twelve months of your utility bills, because array size should follow actual consumption rather than roof area.

The company keeps permitting in-house: the city application, the Austin Energy interconnection request and the rebate filing are prepared by the same person who scoped your system, so questions from the reviewer do not bounce back to you. Installations are done by employees rather than subcontracted crews.

Services

  • Home solar design and installation

    Site assessment, production modelling, equipment selection and installation for pitched and low-slope residential roofs.

  • Battery backup

    Battery sizing and critical-load panel wiring so selected circuits keep running during an outage.

  • Permits and interconnection

    City permit application, utility interconnection paperwork and rebate filing prepared and tracked through approval.

  • Monitoring and warranty service

    Production monitoring setup and warranty repairs on systems the company installed.

Questions answered by Bright Ridge Solar

How long does approval take in Austin?

Permit and interconnection review usually runs three to six weeks. Installation is scheduled once the permit is issued, and the system is energised after the final inspection and utility sign-off.

Can you install on a roof that needs replacing?

Not directly. If the roof has under about five years of life left, the array is quoted after the new roof so panels are not removed and remounted later.

What happens if production drops?

Monitoring alerts are reviewed by the office. Systems installed by Bright Ridge are covered by a workmanship warranty, and the terms are written into the contract.
