About

Ledgerpoint Accounting keeps the monthly books for small businesses in Austin: restaurants, trades, agencies and independent professionals. Work is billed as a flat monthly fee that includes the annual return, so nothing arrives as a surprise invoice in April.

Clients send documents through a shared folder and get a reconciled set of statements by the tenth of the following month. The same accountant handles your file year-round, which matters when a lender or landlord asks for figures at short notice.

Services

  • Monthly bookkeeping

    Transaction categorisation, bank and card reconciliation, and a monthly profit and loss, balance sheet and cash summary.

  • Business tax preparation

    Federal returns for sole proprietors, partnerships, LLCs and S corporations, including quarterly estimate calculations.

  • Payroll

    Pay runs, withholding, contractor payments and year-end forms.

  • Catch-up bookkeeping

    Reconstruction of books that are months or years behind, priced as a one-off project before monthly service starts.

How they work

  • Flat monthly fee that includes the annual business return
  • Statements delivered by the tenth of each month
  • Same accountant on your file all year

Questions answered by Ledgerpoint Accounting

Which software do you work in?

Cloud accounting platforms with bank feeds. If you already use one, the practice works in yours rather than migrating you.

My books are two years behind. Is that a problem?

No. Catch-up work is quoted separately as a project, and monthly service starts once the file is current.

Do you handle sales tax?

Yes, for Texas filers, as part of the monthly scope where it applies.
