About

Highline Digital works with service businesses that sell within a driving radius: trades, clinics, repair shops. The work is mostly unglamorous and measurable, which is the point — profile completeness, location pages that answer real questions, consistent business information across the directories that feed Maps.

Engagements start with a two-week audit that produces a prioritised list. Clients own their accounts, domains and analytics properties throughout, and everything transfers on request without a handover fee.

Services

  • Local search audit

    Two-week review of profile, website, listings and competitors, delivered as a prioritised action list.

  • Google Business Profile management

    Setup, verification, category and service configuration, photos, posts and review response workflow.

  • Location page development

    Service and city pages written for real search intent rather than keyword permutations.

  • Website builds

    Fixed-scope small business websites built for speed and conversion tracking.

How they work

  • Clients own their ad accounts, domains and analytics from day one
  • Audit first, retainer only if the work justifies it
  • No automated location pages built from city lists
