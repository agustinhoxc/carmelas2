About

Riverbank Media runs paid search and social campaigns for small businesses, most of them spending between one and ten thousand dollars a month on media. At that size the biggest wins usually come from conversion tracking that is actually correct and from turning off what is not working quickly.

Campaign setup includes tracking implementation, and the media budget is always invoiced separately from the agency fee so the split stays visible. Evening calls are available for owners who cannot talk during the working day.

Services

  • Campaign setup

    Account structure, keyword and audience research, ad creative and landing page recommendations.

  • Conversion tracking

    Implementation and verification of call, form and purchase tracking before spend starts.

  • Ongoing campaign management

    Budget pacing, bid adjustments, creative testing and monthly reporting.

  • Landing pages

    Focused pages built for specific campaigns rather than sending traffic to a homepage.
