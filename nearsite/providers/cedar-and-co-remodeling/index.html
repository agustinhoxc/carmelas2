About

Cedar & Co Remodeling only does bathrooms. That narrow focus means the crew is on its own schedule rather than waiting behind a larger project, and most jobs run three to five weeks from demolition to final inspection.

A growing share of the work is accessible design: curbless showers, blocking for grab bars, wider doorways and step-free entries planned so the room still looks like a bathroom rather than a hospital. Waterproofing is inspected and photographed before tile goes on.

Services

  • Full bathroom remodel

    Demolition, waterproofing, tile, fixtures, ventilation and lighting in bathrooms of any size.

  • Accessible bathroom conversion

    Curbless showers, grab bar blocking, widened doorways and comfort-height fixtures planned for long-term use.

  • Tile and waterproofing

    Membrane systems and tile work, documented with photographs before covering.

  • Powder room updates

    Smaller-scope refreshes of half baths, usually completed within a week.

How they work

  • Bathrooms only, so the crew is not shared with larger builds
  • Waterproofing photographed before tile is installed
  • Accessible layouts designed to look residential, not clinical

Questions answered by Cedar & Co Remodeling

How long does a bathroom take?

Three to five weeks for a full remodel, longer if a fixture on order is delayed. The schedule is agreed before demolition starts.

Can a curbless shower go in an older house?

Usually yes, though it depends on the floor framing. That is checked during the estimate visit before anything is promised.
