About

Rosewood Renovations remodels kitchens in Portland's older housing stock, where the interesting problems are usually behind the plaster: knob-and-tube wiring, undersized panels, floors that have settled an inch across the room.

Estimates are itemised with named allowances for tile, fixtures and appliances, so two bids can be compared line by line. Change orders are priced and signed before work continues, and the crew works a fixed schedule so the household knows when the house is theirs again.

Services

  • Kitchen remodeling

    Layout changes, cabinetry, counters, lighting and appliance installation, from a refresh to a full gut.

  • Electrical and plumbing upgrades

    Panel upgrades, rewiring of knob-and-tube circuits and supply line replacement carried out as part of the remodel.

  • Structural repair

    Floor levelling, joist sistering and beam work in houses that have settled.

  • Permits and inspections

    Permit applications filed and inspections scheduled by the contractor.

How they work

Questions answered by Rosewood Renovations

What is the current lead time?

Kitchen projects are generally booked six to ten weeks out. Design work can start earlier than the build slot.

Will I have a working kitchen during the remodel?

A temporary sink and appliance setup is included in the scope on full gut projects, usually in an adjacent room.

Who handles permits?

The contractor files the permit and schedules inspections. Fees are shown as a separate line in the estimate.
