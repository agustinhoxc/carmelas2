About

Copper Creek Energy works on commercial arrays: flat-roof warehouses, office buildings, cold storage and agricultural structures across Central Texas. Projects typically start at 50 kW.

Proposals include a production model, a demand-charge analysis against your current billing profile, and the financing structures the company can arrange. The engineering, structural review and interconnection filing are managed as one scope so a single team is accountable through commissioning.

Services

  • Commercial rooftop arrays

    Ballasted and mechanically attached systems for flat and low-slope commercial roofs, with structural review included.

  • Ground-mount and canopy systems

    Field arrays and parking canopies where roof area is limited or unsuitable.

  • Commercial battery storage

    Storage sized against demand charges and critical loads, with controls configured for peak shaving.

  • Financing coordination

    Preparation of the documentation lenders and lease providers require, and comparison of the resulting cash flows.

How they work

  • Proposals include a demand-charge analysis, not only annual production
  • Structural review carried out before the array layout is fixed
  • One project manager from design through commissioning

Questions answered by Copper Creek Energy

What size projects do you take?

Typically 50 kW and above. Smaller commercial roofs are referred out rather than quoted.

How is a commercial quote structured?

As installed cost, modelled annual production, and the effect on both energy and demand charges, so it can be compared against your current bills.
