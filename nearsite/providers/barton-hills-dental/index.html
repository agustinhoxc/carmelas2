About

Barton Hills Dental is a general practice treating adults and children in south Austin. Routine care, fillings, crowns and extractions are handled in-house; complex surgical cases are referred with the imaging already prepared.

The schedule holds slots each morning for urgent problems, and two evenings a week run late for people who cannot leave work during the day. Treatment plans are given in writing with the insurance estimate and the out-of-pocket figure side by side.

Services

  • Routine exams and cleanings

    Preventive visits for adults and children, including X-rays where indicated.

  • Fillings and crowns

    Restorative treatment for decay and cracked teeth, with same-visit temporary crowns where possible.

  • Urgent dental visits

    Held slots each morning for pain, swelling and broken teeth.

  • Extractions

    Straightforward extractions in-house; surgical cases referred with imaging prepared.

How they work

  • Urgent slots held every weekday morning
  • Written treatment plans showing insurance estimate and out-of-pocket cost together
  • Appointments in Spanish

Questions answered by Barton Hills Dental

Do you take my insurance?

The practice is in network with several major plans and files claims for out-of-network plans as well. Call with your plan details before booking so the estimate is accurate.

What does a first visit include?

An exam, X-rays where indicated, and a written plan if treatment is needed. A cleaning is usually scheduled at the same visit when time allows.
